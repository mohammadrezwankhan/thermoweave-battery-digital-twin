ONE SCHEMA, FOUR BOUNDARY IDEAS

The boundary is part of the experiment.

MODEL

Transparent enough to interrogate.

01 / ENERGY BALANCE

Cᵢ Ṫᵢ = Qgen,i − Σ Gᵢⱼ(Tᵢ−Tⱼ) − Gb,i(Tᵢ−Tb,i) − Gc,i(Tᵢ−Tc,i)

Each cell is a graph node. Conductive edges, ambient interfaces, and coolant links all expose their sign convention and SI units.

02 / ELECTRICAL HEAT

Heat follows current, SOC, and temperature.

Joule heating is always available; an optional signed entropic term can be enabled. Parameters remain visibly synthetic unless a licensed validation dataset is added.

03 / COOLANT MARCH

The outlet remembers the inlet path.

A finite-capacity flow balance marches coolant through declared cell segments. Reversing the channel order changes the spatial field and is covered by an automated test.

04 / CONTROL

Bounded, rate-limited, observable.

The baseline zonal controller is deterministic. Optional optimization reports solver status and falls back visibly—never silently—when it cannot produce a command.

VERIFICATION

Evidence before adjectives.

Class-based MATLAB tests cover configuration failure modes, equilibrium, relaxation, energy accounting, scalar/vector equivalence, graph permutation behavior, flow reversal, controller constraints, scenario-specific benefit, faults, deterministic seeds, and export traceability.

ARCHITECTURE

One result contract. Multiple consumers.

LIMITATIONS

Useful because its boundaries are explicit.

  • Lumped isothermal cell nodes; no electrochemical diffusion or ageing prediction.
  • Synthetic parameters; no held-out real-cell accuracy evidence.
  • Controller demonstrations are scenario-specific and are not safety certification.
  • Simscape products are installed locally, but structural model generation remains policy-gated and unclaimed.

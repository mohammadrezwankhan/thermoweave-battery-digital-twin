# Code of Conduct

ThermoWeave is a small technical project, and every participant is expected to make collaboration safe, respectful, and evidence-focused.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, inclusive, and constructive.
- Critique ideas, code, and evidence rather than people.
- Explain technical disagreement clearly and remain open to correction.
- Protect private information and do not disclose credentials, license files, or unpublished data.
- Give accurate credit for external sources, tools, and contributions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, doxxing, credential disclosure, and knowingly misleading scientific or security claims are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not report sensitive incidents in a public issue. Use the private security/reporting route documented in `SECURITY.md` and include only the minimum information needed to investigate. Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with a presumption against retaliation.

This code applies in project spaces and whenever someone is representing the project. Maintainers may clarify or update it as the project and its community grow.
